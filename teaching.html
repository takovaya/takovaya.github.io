Teaching

I have been teaching languages since 2020, working with learners from preschool children to older adults. My teaching covers English, German, Latin and Ancient Greek, in both individual and small-group settings, along with introductory courses in Hungarian, French and Modern Greek.

Over six years I have worked through a range of formats — a summer camp, groups, one-to-one lessons, courses for older learners — and arrived at a multilingual, neurolinguistic and communicative approach.

Alongside teaching, I design digital learning materials and interactive courses.
