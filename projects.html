Corpora, learning platforms and spatial tools

Periploi

Annotated corpus of ancient Greek coastal itineraries · in progress

Periploi is a digital humanities project that grew directly out of my thesis research. Its goal is to develop an annotation framework designed specifically for route-based geographical texts, instead of adapting existing cartographic models to material for which they were never intended.
